Sound Layer Plan for MOTION_SUPREME interactions
===============================================

1. baseDrone — persistent ambient drone that fades in when any interaction starts the audio context.
2. interactiveTextHover — triggered while hovering over the MOTION SUPREME interactive letters.
3. folderHover — fades up whenever a 3D folder is hovered in either orbit or aligned mode.
4. folderSelect — short burst when a folder becomes the active selection (click or alignment).
5. edgeScroll — engages while the cursor pressurises the left/right edges to slide through folders.
6. webPulse — activates when the MOTION333 network regenerates or jitters beneath the lineup.
7. orbitMagnet — rises when a folder is magnetised toward the cursor in orbit mode.
8. alignedState — subtle layer that persists while the lineup mode is active.
9. scrollWash — a soft shimmer that responds to any mouse or trackpad scroll gestures.
10. themeToggle — momentary chord shift when the light/dark mode button is pressed.
11. scrollProgress — gentle ticking synced to the custom scrollbar fill as the page advances.
12. scrollUnlock — bloom that plays once the five-second scroll lock releases.

Each layer is currently generated through Web Audio oscillators as placeholders. Replace with provided audio assets later by updating the SoundManager.createLayer configuration.
